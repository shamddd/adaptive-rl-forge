\section{Ablation Studies}
\label{sec:ablations}

We conduct ablation experiments removing individual readiness signals from CARLS:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{w/o Gradient Alignment:} Removing $\text{Sim}_{\text{grad}}$ causes CARLS to trigger RL excursions during periods of severe gradient conflict, increasing variance.
    \item \textbf{w/o Policy Entropy:} Removing entropy monitoring leads to premature entropy collapse.
    \item \textbf{Fixed Schedule (B6 vs B7):} Replacing CARLS dynamic control with a fixed mixture ratio reduces overall compute efficiency.
\end{itemize}
