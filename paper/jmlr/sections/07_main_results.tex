As shown in Table~\ref{tab:baselines}, CARLS (B7) achieves statistically superior Pass@1 accuracy ($47.26\%$) and Pass@4 accuracy ($64.04\%$) compared to standard sequential training ($42.49\%$ Pass@1, $56.46\%$ Pass@4) while consuming $18.7\%$ fewer total FLOPs ($6.10 \times 10^{10}$ vs. $7.50 \times 10^{10}$).

Furthermore, Figure~\ref{fig:plasticity_pred} presents the zero-shot cross-model plasticity prediction performance when predicting RL gain on \texttt{distilgpt2} using a predictor trained exclusively on \texttt{SmolLM-135M} ($R^2 = 0.7632$, Spearman $\rho = 0.8247$).
