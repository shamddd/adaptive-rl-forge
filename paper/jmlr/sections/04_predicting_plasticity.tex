\section{Predicting Reinforcement-Learning Plasticity}
\label{sec:predicting_plasticity}

To determine when a model checkpoint is ready for RL, we extract three key diagnostic readiness signals prior to initiating an RL excursion:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Gradient Alignment ($\cos(\mathbf{g}_{\text{NTP}}, \mathbf{g}_{\text{RL}})$):} Measured as the cosine similarity between the unclipped parameter gradients of the pre-training NTP loss and the policy gradient loss:
    \begin{equation}
        \text{Sim}_{\text{grad}} = \frac{\langle \nabla_\theta \mathcal{L}_{\text{NTP}}, \nabla_\theta \mathcal{L}_{\text{RL}} \rangle}{\|\nabla_\theta \mathcal{L}_{\text{NTP}}\| \|\nabla_\theta \mathcal{L}_{\text{RL}}\|}
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Policy Entropy ($H(\pi_\theta)$):} The average token-level entropy of the policy output distribution over reasoning prompts.
    \item \textbf{Baseline Pass@k Accuracy:} The pre-RL task accuracy evaluating whether the checkpoint possesses minimal foundational skills.
\end{enumerate}

Our empirical plasticity study demonstrates that high gradient alignment combined with non-collapsed policy entropy strongly correlates with subsequent RL accuracy gain.
